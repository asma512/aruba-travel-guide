Welcome to One Happy Island

Experience the pristine white-sand beaches, vibrant local culture, and perfect weather of Aruba.

Eagle Beach

Widely considered one of the best beaches in the world, Eagle Beach boasts powdery white sand and stunning turquoise waters. It is famous for its iconic, twisted Fofoti trees and provides a serene escape for sunbathers and swimmers alike.

Arikok National Park

Taking up nearly 20% of the island, this national park showcases Aruba's rugged desert terrain. Explore dramatic coastlines, hidden natural pools, and ancient cave paintings left by the indigenous Arawak people.

Renaissance Island

A private island paradise famous for its iconic flamingo beach, Renaissance Island offers crystal-clear waters, soft white sand, and a relaxing escape just off the coast of Aruba. Visitors can enjoy snorkeling, lounging, and up-close encounters with flamingos.
